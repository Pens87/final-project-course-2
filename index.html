Introducing Tall Ships

In a time where basic sailboats or big yachts are often the default when people think of boats, it's important to not forget what boats used to look like. Specifically, during the Age of Sail, which stretches from roughly the 1600s to mid-1800s — though sailing ships existed for long before that time period.

While not many original ships still exist, there are plenty of newer ships that are either replicas of ones from history or merely ones that emulate the older style. To appreciate some beautiful ships, enjoy the gallery below, and be sure to read up on two of my favorites on the other pages of this small website.
