Elissa: Galveston, Texas

The Elissa is the Official Tall Ship of Texas and a National Historic Landmark. She wasn't built in Texas, though. She was built in Aberdeen, Scotland, and launched in 1877. She ended up in Galveston in XYEAR, where she remains docked to this day — unless she's off on a day sail or a big trip, like the Sail250 voyage she undertook in Summer 2026.
