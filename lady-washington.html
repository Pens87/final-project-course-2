Lady Washington: Aberdeen, Washington

Lady Washington Details

The Lady Washington is based out of Grays Harbor Historical Seaport. She's a replica of the boat by the same name back in the late 1700s, which was part of the first American voyage to Japan. The modern Lady was launched in 1989 and has been in numerous TV shows and movies, including Pirates of the Caribbean: Curse of the Black Pearl (as the HMS Interceptor). She's also the State Ship.

You can learn more about the Lady on the Seaport's website.
