Lady Washington: Aberdeen, Washington

The Lady Washington is based out of Grays Harbor Historical Seaport. She's a replica of the boat by the same name back in the late 1700s, a boat which was part of the first American voyage to Japan. The modern Lady was launched in XYEAR and has been in numerous TV shows and movies, including Pirates of the Caribbean: Curse of the Black Pearl (as the HMS Interceptor). She's also the Official Tall Ship of Washington.
